# Put miscellaneous files in this folder
In particular:
* Your presentation slides as a PDF.
* Your demo video as an MP4. It must be at least 1280x720 --- 720p --- encoded with the H.264/MPEG-4 AVC codec.

We recommend compressing your video before adding it to your repository (e.g., with [HandBrake](https://handbrake.fr/) or `ffmpeg`), since large files make your repository slow to clone, and GitHub rejects any single file over 100 MB. At 720p, a ~3 minute demo video should come out well under that.
